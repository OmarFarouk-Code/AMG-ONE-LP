Directly derived from the Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 power unit built in Brixworth. The 1.6-liter V6 internal combustion unit features an electrically assisted single turbocharger with a 90 kW MGU-H motor capable of spinning the turbine to 100,000 RPM before exhaust gas pressure builds, eliminating throttle lag entirely.
