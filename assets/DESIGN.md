## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-performance engineering through a dialect of disciplined luxury and motorsport precision. Built around the ethos of hypercar architecture, the interface rejects decorative indulgence, synthetic glows, and ornamental trends in favor of structural clarity, industrial tension, and tactile certainty.

The aesthetic fuses modern minimalism with unapologetic track-bred aggression. High-contrast surfaces, razor-sharp geometric alignment, and calculated typographic scale convey technical dominance without sacrificing luxury refinement. The emotional response is focused, serious, and exhilarating—akin to entering an F1 technical bay or closing the dihedral door of a carbon-fiber monocoque chassis.

Every element operates with purposeful restraint:
- Visual mass is organized strictly along mathematical axes.
- Surface tension is communicated via hairline structural grids rather than heavy elevation shadows.
- Micro-interactions are instantaneous, mechanical, and damped, mirroring calibrated instrumentation.

## Colors

The palette operates under absolute discipline. Dark mode serves as the primary canvas, reflecting carbon-weave cockpits and nighttime aerodynamic testing, while light-mode accents and alternating inverted sections utilize warm ivory to break monotony without eroding tension.

### Core Values
- **Primary Accent (`#9B1B1E` - AMG Deep Motorsport Red):** Reserved strictly for critical state transitions, focal interactive CTAs, and peak performance metrics (e.g., top speed indicators, active aero status). It must never exceed 5% of total viewport area.
- **Secondary (`#C7C9CC` - Liquid Metallic Silver):** Used for micro-borders, 1px structural datum lines, active toggle indicators, and technical diagrams.
- **Tertiary (`#8A8A8E` - Muted Mechanical Gray):** Dedicated to secondary metadata, technical descriptions, non-active states, and unit notations.
- **Neutral Deep (`#0A0A0A` - Obsidian Dark Canvas):** The default environment base. Pure, deep, light-absorbent.
- **Neutral Elevated Surface (`#1C1C1E` - Chassis Surface):** Reserved for technical spec cards, control panels, interactive trays, and floating telemetry modules.
- **Neutral Light Inverse (`#F5F4F2` - Warm Ivory):** Deployed for high-contrast architectural breaks, spec sheet sheets, or inverted editorial callouts.

### Usage Protocol
No multi-stop gradients, neon hues, or diffused blurs are permitted. Transitions between `#0A0A0A` and `#1C1C1E` define planes of depth, while `#C7C9CC` provides laser-scribed structural edges.

## Typography

Typography functions as both visual engineering and editorial voice. Three type families orchestrate tension:

1. **Barlow Condensed (Display & Headlines):** Imposes extreme scale, aggressive velocity, and vertical rhythm. Headlines must maintain tight leading to mimic the compact packaging of an endurance powertrain.
2. **Hanken Grotesk (Editorial & Body Copy):** Provides neutral, humanist legibility at 15–16px with an open 1.6 relative line-height. It grounds the visceral headlines with calm, analytical documentation.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Instrumentation & Eyebrows):** Dedicated to technical telemetry, chassis specs, small-caps section flags, and numerical data. Numerical figures must enforce open-type tabular lining (`tnum`) to ensure telemetry readouts align across comparative columns.

### Editorial Hierarchies
- **Eyebrow Headers:** Transformed to full uppercase with wide letter tracking (`0.15em`). Always paired with sequential numerical indices (e.g., `01 // POWERTRAIN ARCHITECTURE`).
- **Telemetry Readouts:** Large numerical readouts must be accompanied by muted small-scale SI units (`kW`, `Nm`, `G`, `km/h`) placed immediately adjacent or subscripted in `#8A8A8E`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an asymmetric 12-column grid designed to mirror wind-tunnel aerodynamic profiles. Symmetrical layouts are deliberately suppressed in favor of high-tension 8/4 and 7/5 column compositions.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop Grid:** 12 columns, fixed 32px gutters (`2rem`), 64px (`4rem`) outer safe-zone margins. Max-width pegged at 1600px.
- **Asymmetric Partitioning:** Primary media or vehicle framing commands 8 columns; analytical specifications, contextual copy, or interactive readouts occupy the remaining 4 columns. Alternatively, 7/5 splits provide balance for narrative prose paired with vertical telemetry stacks.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Section-to-section clearance ranges rigorously between `96px` (`6rem`) and `160px` (`10rem`). Internal card and module spacing is paced in strict multiples of 8px.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Below 768px, asymmetric columns collapse sequentially into a single column. The 8/4 proportion switches to a vertically stacked layout where telemetry blocks precede body documentation. Gutters tighten to 16px, margins to 20px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and razor-thin metallic framing rather than simulated light projection or blurred drop shadows. The design system rejects organic shadows entirely.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Ground Floor:** `#0A0A0A` serves as the zero-plane foundation.
- **Chassis Planes (Level 1 Surfaces):** Technical panels, active specs, and interactive docks sit at `#1C1C1E`.
- **Structural Separation:** Planes are separated strictly by 1px solid borders rendered in `#C7C9CC` at 20% opacity (`rgba(199, 201, 204, 0.2)`), escalating to 100% solid `#C7C9CC` under hover or active focus states.
- **Backdrop Precision:** When contextual overlays (e.g., flyout engineering schematics, full-screen audio analysis) sit above the canvas, they utilize `#0A0A0A` at 85% opacity paired with a clinical 12px backdrop blur.
- **Ambient Shadow (Exception Only):** To isolate floating HUD modules over high-detail rendering or video backdrops, a single disciplined shadow profile is authorized: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`. No color tinting is permitted.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a strict zero-radius (`0px`) geometry. The AMG ONE represents structural carbon fiber, titanium milling, and aerodynamic splitters—curved, pill-shaped, or soft UI elements contradict this engineering reality.

### Structural Geometries
- **Containers, Buttons, Badges, and Input Elements:** Absolute right-angle construction (`border-radius: 0px`).
- **Chamfers & Angled Details:** Selected structural surfaces (such as status badges or primary CTA corners) may employ a 45-degree mechanical chamfer cut (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) measuring precisely 8px, evoking specialized machined components.
- **Hairlines:** Dividing rules must never exceed 1px physical width.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Track Red):** Rectangular, zero-radius. Background `#9B1B1E`, text `#F5F4F2`, typography `JetBrains Mono` 12px bold, small-caps, tracked at `0.1em`. Hover state initiates an instant inverted transition: background `#C7C9CC`, text `#0A0A0A`. No transitional delay over 150ms.
- **Secondary Action (Technical Ghost):** 1px border `#C7C9CC` at 40% opacity, transparent background, text `#F5F4F2`. Hover scales border opacity to 100% with a background fill of `#1C1C1E`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Text-only with leading zero sequence index (e.g., `[ 01 EXPLORE AERODYNAMICS ]`), hover state triggers a solid `#9B1B1E` 2px bottom rule.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Zero radius, 1px border in `#C7C9CC` at 20% opacity.
- Monospace typographic readouts (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px).
- Status active variant incorporates a static 4px solid `#9B1B1E` square indicator at the leading edge.

### Spec & Telemetry Cards
- Surface: `#1C1C1E`.
- Framing: 1px hairline border in `#C7C9CC` at 15% opacity.
- Composition: Top-aligned eyebrow category in `#8A8A8E`, large tabular numerical readouts centered or left-aligned, followed by micro-label metadata at the foot.
- Hover behavior: Border transitions from 15% opacity to `#C7C9CC` 60% opacity with zero shift in element position or elevation lift.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Base: `#0A0A0A` interior with a single 1px bottom rule in `#8A8A8E`.
- Focus State: Bottom rule snaps to 2px `#9B1B1E`; label translates vertically with tracking expanding to `0.2em`.
- Checkboxes/Radios: Precise 14x14px square boxes. Active state features an inset 8x8px `#9B1B1E` solid square. No rounded toggle switches.

### Specialized Component: Technical Telemetry Bar
- Dedicated horizontal instrumentation component docked to section anchors.
- Features real-time parameter blocks (e.g., `MGU-K OUTPUT`, `DOWNFORCE @ 200 KM/H`, `BOOST PRESSURE`).
- Uses dynamic tabular lining typography, segmented progress indices (10-stage micro tick marks in `#C7C9CC`), and `#9B1B1E` threshold markers.
